If you're wondering

Five short answers before class.

1Will this work in my industry?The question almost everyone asks first.

Your industry sets the numbers and how compensation is structured. Knowing your value and advocating for yourself works whether you're in healthcare, tech, law or banking. The rules change from industry to industry. The woman playing the game is what we work on.

2I hate negotiating. Is that a problem?Short answer: no.

Negotiating isn't asking for a favor. It's a business conversation about the value you create. And your company knows exactly how expensive it would be to replace you, even if nobody ever says it out loud.

3What if they say no?And what if it damages things with my boss?

Then you've learned something. A no tells you what's possible, what needs to happen next and sometimes whether you're in the right place at all. A well-prepared, professional conversation doesn't wreck a healthy relationship.

4Will the inner-work stuff really help my career?A fair question from a room full of rational women.

The identity work is what makes the strategy land. People respond to who's saying the words far more than to the words themselves. You'll see exactly what that shift did for Shelly, Nina and Erica.

5What if I get found out?The one nobody says out loud.

Almost every high achiever I've worked with has felt this, including me, as the highest-paid person in my division. It hits the most capable people hardest. Your results are real. We work on letting you actually own them.
