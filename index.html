The V.O.I.C.E. Framework™

Negotiation is the vehicle. Transformation is the destination.

Most negotiation programs start and end with what to say. This one starts with who's saying it. Because people buy the woman before they buy the words, and the room hears doubt before it hears the script.

V Value

See your value before you ask anyone else to. Companies don't pay for effort. They pay for value.

O Ownership

If you want a bigger job, make your job bigger. You're the CEO of your career.

I Identity

Become the woman first. Your career will rarely exceed the identity you've accepted.

C Communication

Make your value impossible to ignore, without over-explaining or apologizing.

E Execution

Courage in action. Confidence shows up after you do the scary thing. Rarely before.

Meet your host

From bagging groceries at the Acme to the highest-paid person in her division. Man or woman.

I grew up in a house with no savings. At 18, I lost my mother. Soon after, my father declared bankruptcy. I put myself through college, graduated into a recession with $50,000 in student debt, and bagged groceries while I sent J.P. Morgan hundreds of résumés. Eventually they said yes. Probably just to make me stop.

My career took off, and I was underpaid. My first raise negotiation was a disaster. I asked for too little and looked like a rookie doing it. That meeting started a lifelong obsession with negotiation, and more than anything, with the psychology behind it.

Over 26 years at J.P. Morgan, Merrill Lynch/Bank of America and Deutsche Bank, I negotiated high-stakes deals and led global talent initiatives, which means I also sat on the other side of the table deciding who got paid what. Then I got fired two days before my boss retired (a classic Wall Street F.U.) and spent five years in Costa Rica and India doing the inner work nobody on Wall Street teaches.
