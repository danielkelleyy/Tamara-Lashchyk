Meet your host

From bagging groceries to the highest paid person in her division.

I grew up in a house with no savings. At 18, I lost my mother. Soon after, my father declared bankruptcy. I put myself through college, graduated into a recession with $50,000 ($150k in today's money) in student debt and bagged groceries while I sent J.P. Morgan hundreds of résumés. Eventually they said yes. Probably just to make me stop.

My career took off yet I was still underpaid. My first raise negotiation was somewhat of a disaster. I asked for too little and looked like a rookie doing it. That meeting started a lifelong obsession with negotiation and the psychology behind it.

I spent 26 years working at firms like J.P. Morgan, Merrill Lynch/Bank of America and Deutsche Bank negotiating high stakes deals and leading global initiatives. Also, I've sat on both sides of the table deciding who got paid what which gave me a unique insight into how compensation decisions were made.
